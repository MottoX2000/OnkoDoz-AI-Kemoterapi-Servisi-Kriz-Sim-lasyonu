## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines high-stakes oncology incident management through the visual lens of 1970s groove poster design, psychedelic geometric prints, and physical analog mission-control equipment. Rather than falling back on sterile, generic hospital dashboards or run-of-the-mill modern dark developer interfaces, it creates an immersive crisis simulation environment: warm, tangible, tactile, and intellectually engaging.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Brand Personality:** Analytical, nostalgic, deliberate, authoritative yet playfully educational (Socratic Primer). It feels like an illuminated, illustrated computing manual from an alternate 1974 timeline where analog mainframe consoles met funk poster pop-art.
- **Target Audience:** Software engineers, oncology IT specialists, QA automators, and DevSecOps practitioners navigating medical dosing failure drills under pressure.
- **Emotional Response:** Sharp operational urgency balanced with deep psychological safety; structured tactile reassurance rather than cold anxiety; tactile joy from physical buttons, thick borders, and offset block shadows.

### Core Visual Language
- **1970s Chevron Stripes & Header Banners:** Multi-toned arching chevrons (burnt terracotta, golden amber, mustard, and deep plum) anchoring screen headers and milestone thresholds.
- **Tactile Neo-Brutalist Surfaces:** Warm vanilla/cream parchment backgrounds layered with chunky cards framed in deep plum-chocolate outlines (`#2B1829`), reinforced with sharp hard-drop box shadows (`4px 4px 0px`).
- **Telemetry & Socratic Divergence:** Crisp terminal-style CRT consoles nestled in deep plum boxes with glowing amber telemetry lines, complemented by calm lavender/retro-indigo dialog drawers that channel the introspective wisdom of the Socratic AI Tutor.

## Colors

The palette draws its foundation directly from 1970s print poster art: warm parchment bases, goldenrod accents, burnt terracotta, and deep plum line work. These tones are engineered into clear functional roles for the oncology crisis flow.

### Functional Roles & Hierarchies
- **Parchment Canvas (`vanilla-base` #FBF2C4 & `vanilla-card` #FFF9E6):** Sets a warm, natural backdrop that eliminates screen fatigue and evokes vintage technical textbooks and printed simulation field guides.
- **Outline & High-Contrast Ink (`ink-plum` #2B1829):** Replaces pure black with a rich, ultra-dense plum-chocolate. Used for all card borders (2.5px), typography, and hard drop-shadows to preserve 70s silk-screen print fidelity.
- **Operational Amber (`mustard-gold` #FDB827 / #F5A623):** Serves as primary interactive call-to-action buttons, active badges, and the 60-second countdown timer.
- **Terracotta Alert (`burnt-orange` #E05A47):** Used for urgent crisis flags, secondary chevron bands, and critical patient alerts.
- **Socratic Wisdom (`socratic-indigo` #5E548E & `socratic-teal` #4D7796):** Deliberately departs from the warm emergency spectrum. When the AI Tutor is active or the timer is paused, cool indigo and soft lavender surfaces (`socratic-surface` #ECE8F4) create a calming space for analytical reflection.
- **Telemetry Chamber (`telemetry-bg` #1F121E):** A deep vintage CRT screen container that frames live server logs, Redis alerts, and audit traces in high-contrast monospaced amber and emerald.

## Typography

The typographical pairing resolves the tension between retro poster styling and medical telemetry requirements:

1. **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Features rhythmic, quirky ink traps and tight geometric turns reminiscent of 1970s structural titling. Used for titles, stage cards, decision option headers, and banner tags. Set with slight negative tracking on display sizes for a printed poster feel.
2. **Body & Pedagogical Prose (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly geometry and open counters, ensuring high legibility for complex clinical narratives, causal feedback ("Ne Oldu / Neden Oldu"), and Socratic prompts.
3. **Telemetry & Countdown (JetBrains Mono):** Injects computing precision. Handles real-time system metrics, Redis key outputs, SQL transaction IDs, and the fixed-width 60-second crisis clock.

## Layout & Spacing

The simulation runs primarily within a mobile-first viewport (`375px` to `440px` max-width container), centered gracefully with retro margins on desktop viewports.

### Rhythm & Proportions
- **4px/8px Baseline Unit:** Component paddings and stack gaps snap to multi-units of 4px and 8px (`space-sm: 8px`, `space-md: 16px`, `space-lg: 20px`).
- **Mobile Container Shell:** On desktop, the screen renders inside a framed vintage pocket console (`max-width: 440px`, centered, minimum viewport height `100dvh`, bordered with deep plum framing and an exterior warm terracotta backdrop).
- **Sticky Crisis Ribbon:** A 54px high top bar fixed at the viewport ceiling containing the crisis step breadcrumb, current simulation clock (`08:15`), and the countdown timer pill.
- **Bottom Socratic Anchorage:** The collapsible AI Tutor dock rests at the base of the frame, preserving 80px of clear scrolling space above so primary decision CTAs are never obscured.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred Gaussian drop-shadows and glassmorphism entirely, utilizing tactile **Hard Neo-Retro Drop-Shadows** and bold ink outlines inspired by mid-70s screen printing and physical analog push buttons.

### Shadow & Border Specifications
- **Ink Border Rule:** All interactive cards, inputs, and chips possess a `2.5px solid #2B1829` border.
- **Base Level Cards (Surface 1):** Offset shadow of `4px 4px 0px #2B1829`. Background is warm paper `#FFF9E6`.
- **Primary Interactive Elements & Decision Buttons:** Offset shadow of `4px 4px 0px #2B1829`. On `:hover` or touch active state, translate `translate(2px, 2px)` with shadow reduced to `2px 2px 0px #2B1829`. On `:pressed`, translate `translate(4px, 4px)` with zero shadow, providing tactile mechanical feedback.
- **Drawer & Socratic Drawer (Surface 2):** Inverted top offset shadow `0px -4px 0px #2B1829` with `2.5px solid #2B1829` outer rim, establishing an elevated physical overlay sheet.
- **Telemetry Console (Recessed Inset):** Recessed inner look using an inset border or deep contrasting surface (`#1F121E`) without exterior shadow, visually sinking it into the card face like an integrated CRT screen.

## Shapes

The shape system balances groovy geometric curves with sturdy structural cards:
- **Cards and Major Panels:** `12px` to `16px` border-radius combined with thick `2.5px` plum strokes.
- **Pill Badges & Countdown Timers:** Full stadium pill radii (`9999px`) for time capsules, status indicators, and question category selectors.
- **Option Letter Circles:** Perfect circles (`32px x 32px`, `50%` radius) bordered with `2px solid #2B1829`.
- **Top Chevron Arch Motif:** Repeated 4-tier geometric chevron headers with downward angled stripes (14px stripe height, angled at 15 degrees) framing the top of every key screen.

## Components

### 1. Retro Chevron Banner Header
- **Structure:** 4 concentric angled stripes running across the top container (`#E05A47`, `#F5A623`, `#FDB827`, `#F7C1BB`) bordered along the lower edge by a `2.5px` solid plum line.
- **Status Overlay:** Floats the hospital unit title `"YZM301 · ONKODOZ-AI"` and live incident time badge (`08:15 · 48 HASTA BEKLİYOR`).

### 2. Decision Action Cards (`data-cy="option-a-btn"`, `data-cy="option-b-btn"`)
- **Structure:** Full width, minimum height `68px`, flex row layout, `14px 16px` padding.
- **Color Discipline:** Before a user selects, both options MUST share an identical neutral retro palette: `#FFF9E6` background, `2.5px solid #2B1829` border, and `4px 4px 0px #2B1829` shadow. Neither option may indicate correctness via early color hints.
- **Option Badge:** Left-aligned `32px` circular pill containing bold "A" or "B" in `Space Grotesk`.
- **Interaction:** Mechanical press down effect on tap (`translate(3px, 3px)`).

### 3. Role Telemetry Box (`data-cy="role-telemetry"`)
- **Visuals:** Recessed vintage mainframe terminal box. Surface `#1F121E`, text color `#FDB827` (amber phosphor), with a `3px` solid `#E05A47` vertical accent on the left border.
- **Typography:** `JetBrains Mono` 12px with monospace data chips (e.g. `[REDIS-WARN]`, `ttl=-1`, `db_pool=94/100`).

### 4. Countdown Timer Capsule (`data-cy="timer-display"`)
- **Structure:** Pill container (`rounded-full`), padded with `6px 14px`.
- **Normal State:** Background `#FDB827`, border `2px solid #2B1829`, text `#2B1829` bold monospace (`⏱ 00:48`).
- **Paused State (`data-cy="timer-paused-badge"`):** Shifts to `#5E548E` background with `#FFF9E6` text, displaying `"⏸ SAYAÇ DURAKLATILDI"`.

### 5. Causal Feedback Card ("Ne Oldu / Neden Oldu")
- **Structure:** Dynamic reveal card placed directly beneath the selected decision option.
- **Aesthetic:** Double-decker vintage note card with a thick dividing line:
  - Top Deck ("NE OLDU?"): Highlighted with a pastel peach tag (`#F7C1BB`), summarising the operational consequence.
  - Bottom Deck ("NEDEN OLDU?"): Highlighted with a vintage gold tag (`#FDB827`), articulating the software engineering principle (e.g. *Strong Consistency vs Eventual Consistency*).
  - Next CTA Button (`data-cy="next-stage-btn"`): Full width `#F5A623` button with bold text and right chevron.

### 6. Socratic AI Tutor Panel (`data-cy="tutor-input"`, `data-cy="tutor-response"`)
- **Aesthetic:** Soft vintage lavender container (`#ECE8F4`) framed in `2.5px solid #5E548E` with an inverted top shadow.
- **Header:** Features retro icon, Socratic mascot badge, and title: *"Sokratik AI Tutor · Cevabı Söylemez, Düşündürür"*.
- **Speech Bubble:** Chunky white speech bubble (`#FFFFFF`) with a small triangular directional tail pointing to the Tutor avatar, bordered in `#2B1829`.
- **Input Field:** Cream-toned text area (`#FFF9E6`) with `2px solid #2B1829` outline, companion pill send button (`data-cy="tutor-send-btn"`).

### 7. Selection Chips & Radio Pills (Profile Onboarding)
- **Normal:** Background `#FFF9E6`, border `2px solid #2B1829`, text `#2B1829`.
- **Selected:** Background `#F5A623`, hard shadow `2px 2px 0px #2B1829`, bold typography with a vintage circular checkmark.
